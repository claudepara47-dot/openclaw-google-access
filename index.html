OpenClaw Personal Google Access

This is the information website for a personal Google Calendar and Google Drive integration operated by claudepara47-dot. It connects the operator’s own Google account to their locally operated OpenClaw assistant through gogcli.

What the integration does

  • Reads calendars and events, and creates, updates or deletes calendar entries when instructed.
  • Finds and reads Drive files, and uploads, edits or deletes files when instructed and permitted by the Google account.
  • Uses the configured assistant model and messaging channel to process requests and return results.

This deployment is for the operator’s personal use. It is not a public sign-in service, and this static website does not connect to Google accounts or collect Google data.

Authorization and control

Google authorization is completed through Google’s consent screen. The operator can revoke this integration at any time in Google Account connections.

This is an independently operated integration, not an official Google service or the official OpenClaw project website.
